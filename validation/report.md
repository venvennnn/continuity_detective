# Wardrobe Capability Validation

Status: **Real reference-transition checks pass; automatic foreground occlusion remains a documented limitation handled through explicit human assessment. Not a validated general wardrobe detector.**

This application measures torso colour appearance. It does not infer garment identity, fabric, missing layers, or story causality. Thresholds are provisional. Body landmark visibility does not establish that clothing is unobstructed.

Deterministic tests cover reference boundaries, crop transport, colour measurements, unusable inputs, and source timestamp extraction. Passing these does not validate wardrobe detection on real footage.

Required real-footage cases: unchanged clothing under pose/light variation; visible wardrobe colour change; deliberate baseline update; later unwanted change; blur; foreground torso occlusion; small subject; multiple-person ambiguity. Record source provenance, expected/actual results, raw scores/quality measurements, thresholds, false positives/misses, and reference IDs here as each is actually checked. Separate calibration from held-out examples where possible.

Do not claim completion or accuracy from placeholder cases or synthetic colour patches.

## Discovered issues and repairs

- Requiring full hips rejected cropped seated subjects. Upper-torso geometry now uses visible shoulders and the upper half of the estimated torso axis, preserving off-frame/ambiguity rejection and mask quality checks. This is a region proposal, not proof of visible clothing.
- Average Laplacian variance confused clear white fabric with blur. A strongest-edge focus measurement inside the eroded valid mask supplements average texture. Provisional tail threshold 15 was calibrated against these original frames and heavy Gaussian-blur augmentations, not independent footage. Mild blur remains texture-dependent.
- Effective mask hashes and measured quality provenance now participate in comparison identity, so changed masks cannot silently retain an old review decision.
- Preparation now assesses the exact default rectangle that confirmation will use, rather than a different proposal polygon.

## Blocking occlusion finding

A read-only audit applied uniform/textured synthetic foreground objects over most of a torso. Pose visibility/person coverage remained high and the quality gate passed these cases. This is a genuine automatic-occlusion limitation. Blur checks cannot solve it. The proposed human Not assessable assessment is documented in devpost/occlusion-proposal.md and was accepted when the learner instructed proceeding with the workbench. Natural motion/occlusion, independent calibration and held-out performance remain pending.
